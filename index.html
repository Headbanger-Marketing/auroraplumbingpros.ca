FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Pricing, licensing, emergencies, and the water itself, covered below.

Are you licensed and insured for plumbing work in Aurora?

Licensed, insured, and working under permits through the local authority whenever the code requires them, so every Aurora job leaves a paper trail that insurers and buyers respect.

What counts as a plumbing emergency worth calling about?

Actively leaking water, sewage backing into the home, or no water at all. Aurora dispatch treats those as priority calls at any hour, day or weekend.

Can you swap a water heater in one visit?

In most Aurora homes, yes, since the standard configurations travel on our trucks, and venting or supply adjustments are quoted before the work begins rather than discovered mid-job.

Our bungalow still has galvanized. Full repipe or partial?

Depends on where the rust is winning. One discoloured fixture can be a branch repair, but house-wide low pressure means the Aurora answer is a full, permitted repipe.

Do you work on well systems around Aurora?

The rural routes north and east of town are familiar territory, covering pressure tanks, iron and hardness treatment, UV systems, and winterization for seasonal and full-time properties.

Is town water here actually hard?

Hard enough to scale fixtures and kettle elements steadily. Most Aurora homes see a clear difference in showers, glassware, and appliance life once a metered softener goes in.

How does pricing work before you start?

A site visit, then a written quote with scope and price, free and without obligation. Aurora customers approve the number before any work begins, and invoices match the approval.

Do you go outside Aurora for calls?

Yes, regularly to Newmarket, Oak Ridges, King City, Sharon, and Schomberg, in addition to Aurora itself.

Do you serve areas outside of Aurora, Ontario?

Yes. We serve Aurora and nearby communities including Newmarket, Oak Ridges, King City, Sharon, and the surrounding area.
