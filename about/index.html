Can aging pipes be replaced while protecting finished rooms?

A repiping plan in Aurora should begin with the pipe material, the locations of past leaks, and the areas of the home that must remain undisturbed. If a property has galvanized piping, aging copper, or legacy plastic, document visible pipe markings and photograph accessible runs, fittings, and damage. For a renovated home, identify plaster, hardwood, millwork, and finished ceilings to protect. Has flooring or a ceiling changed without an obvious leak?

When a hidden leak is suspected in Aurora, record when signs first appeared, whether they change with water use, and which rooms or fixtures are nearby. Photograph staining, cupped flooring, damp areas, and meter readings if accessible. If the home has radiant heating, a long private water service, a well connection, or finished lower levels, mention those details because they affect which sections should be tested.
